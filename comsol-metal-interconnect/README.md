# VLSI Metal Interconnect Simulation

Modelled a two-layer nanoscale metal interconnect using COMSOL Multiphysics 6.0 as part of VLSI coursework at VIT Vellore.

## Objective

To study the electrical and thermal behaviour of metal interconnects and examine the effects of current flow and Joule heating.

## Simulation

The model uses Electric Currents, Electrostatics and Heat Transfer in Solids, with Electromagnetic Heating coupling.

The study examines electric potential distribution, electric field concentration and temperature distribution around the interconnect junction.

## Results

The simulation report shows localised electric field concentration and heating near the interconnect junction.

## Tools

COMSOL Multiphysics 6.0
